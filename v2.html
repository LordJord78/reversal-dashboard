v2 — the graded reversal

loading…

Research, not the traded strategy. v1 asks a yes/no question: did the prior session move at least 2 standard deviations? If yes, take the opposite side at 2x; if no, do nothing. That fires about 16 times a year. v2 asks how much — position is −z × 0.25 every session, capped at 2x, with no threshold at all. A z of 1.9 is not discarded, just held smaller than a z of 2.4.

Same instruments, same open→close execution, same no-overnight rule. The only change is that the gate becomes a dial. Positions below 0.02x are rounded away as too small to be worth an order.

The verdict, up front: v2 trades 15× more often and does not make more money.

It wins on Sharpe only below roughly 1bp of slippage. Above that v1 wins and the gap widens fast, because v2 pays execution 251 times a year for an edge 33× smaller per trade. The lower drawdown is a position-sizing effect, not a structural one — scaled to v1's risk, its worst day is identical. Full working in RESEARCH_V2.md.

Why the threshold exists

The most useful thing this study produced is a fact about v1, not v2: the |z| ≥ 2 threshold is a cost filter, not a signal filter. Sub-threshold sessions carry real predictive content — that is why v2 works at all before costs. What the threshold does is concentrate trading into the sessions where the expected move is large enough to pay for crossing the spread.

So v1's sparseness isn't the strategy being picky about signal quality. It's the strategy refusing to trade when the prize is smaller than the toll.

Walk-forward

Two independent halves, no reoptimisation. Both survive, so the structure is real — it is only the cost arithmetic that sinks it. The v2 portfolio was the most stable thing tested.

How to settle it

One measurement decides this, and you are already instrumented to take it. Log real fills on the signals page. Entry is meant to be the session open and exit the close — the gap between those and what you actually got is your slippage.

Under 1bp → v2 is genuinely better: same Sharpe, a third of the drawdown, something to do most days instead of fourteen times a year.
Over 1bp → v1 is better and this page stays research.
