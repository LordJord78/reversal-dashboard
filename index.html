Session reversal signals

The rule: when an instrument's prior session moves open→close by 2 or more standard deviations of its own 60-day range, take the opposite side at the next open and close it at that close. Same day, no overnight hold.

Below, z is how many standard deviations yesterday actually moved, and trigger is how far it would have had to move to fire. Anything at |z| ≥ 2 is a trade. Past alerts and their fills live on the trade alerts page.

Control — must show no edge

TLT and GLD are the instruments that proved this signal isn't a data artifact. They do trigger — z is scaled by each instrument's own volatility, so they fire about as often as QQQ does. What makes them controls is that trading those triggers earns nothing: SR −0.17 and −0.09 across the full history. The failure mode to watch for isn't a fire, it's a control quietly developing real edge — that would mean the signal is picking up something in the data rather than in the market. Never trade these.
